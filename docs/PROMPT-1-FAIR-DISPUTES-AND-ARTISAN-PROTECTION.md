# Prompt 1 (Updated): Fair Disputes and Artisan Protection

Continue SkillConnect-HUB. Do not add loans, booking, or any completion code. Make refunds and disputes fair to artisans and customers, using evidence. Do not add anything I did not ask for.

## 1. Confirming an Order (Keep It Simple)

- After the artisan marks an order as done or delivered, the customer sees a **Confirm and release payment** button. One tap releases the money to the artisan. No code and no extra steps.
- If the customer does nothing for 48 hours after **done**, release the payment automatically. Send a reminder to the customer at 24 hours and 6 hours before.

## 2. Artisan Proof

- To mark an order as done, the artisan must upload at least one photo or a short video of the finished work or delivered item, using the in-app upload. Save it with date and time.
- Proof is visible to the customer and admin.

## 3. Customer Dispute Rules

- A customer can open a dispute only after **done** and before confirming or the 48 hours end.
- Required: choose a reason (**not done**, **not as agreed**, **damaged**, **wrong item**), write what exactly is wrong (minimum 30 characters), and upload at least one photo or video.
- Opening a dispute holds the money. The artisan can reply with a written answer and more proof within 48 hours.
- A new account can open at most 2 disputes per month.

## 4. Decisions

- Admin sees: the quote, chat, payment, artisan proof, customer proof, and both statements.
- Options: full refund, part refund (set amount), release to the artisan, or ask the artisan to fix the work first with a deadline.
- If the artisan's proof is clear and the customer gives no real evidence, rule for the artisan.
- Both sides get a written reason for the decision. Add one **appeal** per dispute, reviewed by a different admin.

## 5. Deposits and Cancellations

- Artisan can set a deposit percent on a quote for materials. The deposit is not refunded if the customer cancels after the artisan started work.
- Allow milestones (**deposit**, **middle**, **final**) on larger quotes.
- Limit free changes: the artisan sets how many changes are included in the quote.

## 6. Strikes and Trust

- A dispute decided as false or unsupported adds a strike to the customer. 1 strike: warning. 2 strikes: lower limit per order. 3 strikes: admin reviews for suspension.
- Fake proof from an artisan adds a strike with the same steps.
- Show artisans a short customer history before they accept a job: number of completed orders and number of disputes lost. New customers show **New customer**.
- New customers have a lower maximum order amount until they complete a few honest orders.
- Artisans can decline a job with no penalty.

## 7. Rules Page

- Add a simple rules page for both sides in plain words, linked near the pay button.

- Run the build.
